Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 14, 2026
Last Updated: June 11, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how moteroon ("Application", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when the Application is used. moteroon is a personal TikTok account management tool that uses official TikTok APIs to upload content, review published content, and analyze account performance.

2. Information We Collect

The Application may collect and process the following information after authorization through TikTok Login Kit:

  • TikTok OAuth access tokens and refresh tokens
  • TikTok Open ID and account identifiers
  • Profile information provided through TikTok APIs
  • Account statistics provided through TikTok APIs
  • Video information and content metadata
  • Video files selected for upload

3. How We Use Information

The information collected is used solely to:

  • Authenticate requests to TikTok APIs
  • Upload and publish video content
  • Retrieve account profile information
  • Retrieve account statistics and analytics
  • Retrieve information about published videos
  • Manage the authenticated TikTok account

Information is not sold, rented, or used for advertising purposes.

4. Data Storage and Security

Authentication tokens and application data may be stored locally on authorized devices used to access the Application. Reasonable technical and administrative measures are implemented to protect information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. Data transmitted between the Application and TikTok services is transferred using secure HTTPS / TLS connections.

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

The Application does not sell or share personal information with third parties. Information may be transmitted to TikTok solely as required to provide Application functionality through official TikTok APIs.

6. Third-Party Services

The Application uses TikTok Login Kit and TikTok APIs. TikTok may independently collect and process information in accordance with its own policies.

TikTok Privacy Policy: https://www.tiktok.com/legal/page/us/privacy-policy/en

7. Data Retention

Authentication tokens are retained only as long as necessary to maintain authorized access. Stored information may be deleted at any time by removing local application data or revoking authorization through TikTok account settings.

8. User Rights

Users may request deletion of locally stored information at any time. Users may also revoke Application access through their TikTok account settings.

9. Children's Privacy

The Application is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. International Data Transfers

Information may be transferred to TikTok systems located in various countries. By using the Application, users acknowledge that such transfers may occur as required to provide Application functionality.

11. Security Incident Response

In the event of suspected credential compromise, users should immediately revoke all OAuth tokens via TikTok account settings, delete the local credential files, and re-authenticate before resuming use of the Application.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document.

13. Governing Law

This Privacy Policy is governed by the laws of Japan. Personal data processing also complies with the Act on the Protection of Personal Information (APPI) of Japan to the extent applicable.
