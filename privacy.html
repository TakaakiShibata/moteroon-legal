Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 14, 2026
Last Updated: May 18, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how moteroon ("Application", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when the Developer uses the Application. The Application is a personal automation tool used exclusively by its Developer to post video content to TikTok.

2. Information We Collect

The Application collects and processes the following information:

  • TikTok OAuth Access Token: An access token obtained through TikTok's OAuth 2.0 authentication flow. This token is stored locally on the Developer's device.
  • TikTok Open ID: A unique identifier assigned by TikTok to the Developer's account, used for API authentication.
  • Video content: Pre-recorded video files selected by the Developer for upload to TikTok.

3. How We Use Information

The information collected is used exclusively for:

  • Authenticating API requests to TikTok on behalf of the Developer
  • Uploading video content to the Developer's TikTok account

No information is used for advertising, analytics, or any commercial purpose.

4. Data Storage and Security

All data, including access tokens, is stored locally on the Developer's personal device. No data is stored on external servers operated by the Developer. Data is transmitted only to TikTok's official API endpoints using HTTPS encryption.

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise share any data with third parties. Data is transmitted only to TikTok's API as necessary to provide the Application's functionality.

6. Third-Party Services

The Application uses TikTok's Content Posting API. TikTok may collect and process data in accordance with their own Privacy Policy, available at: https://www.tiktok.com/legal/page/us/privacy-policy/en

7. Data Retention

Access tokens are retained locally on the Developer's device until they expire (typically 24 hours) or are manually deleted. Refresh tokens may be retained for up to 365 days to allow re-authentication without repeating the OAuth flow.

8. User Rights

As the sole user of this Application, the Developer has full control over all data, including the right to delete access tokens at any time by removing the token file from their device.

9. Children's Privacy

The Application is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. International Data Transfers

Data may be transferred to TikTok's servers located outside Japan. By using the Application, the Developer consents to such transfers.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated at any time. The "Last Updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes.
